WHAT YOU ARE SEEING

Four generated chamber surfaces and simplified great vessels. This model is a schematic, not a patient reconstruction or an atlas-derived heart.

Measurements are calculated from the model mesh in arbitrary units (u), not millimetres or clinical chamber volumes.

Next step: a segmented CT/MRI surface mesh would replace this schematic with actual anatomy. The 2D CardioMorph study does not provide a patient-specific 3D reconstruction.
